How we work

Before starting, we talk with you about exactly what you expect — a basic window clean with a simple track wipe, or deeper detail like screens cleaned, thorough tracks, and skylights. We only do what you ask for. If you don’t want tracks touched, we won’t touch them.

Professional cleaning inside and out with traditional tools — squeegee, mop, T-bar — plus ladders. A water-fed pole with purified deionized water is available if requested in advance. Same-day booking is sometimes possible but never guaranteed; best to call so we can personally check the schedule.

🎮 Play: The Window Cleaning Game — Walk Around in First Person

You start in first person — right through the cleaner's eyes, like virtual reality. Walk up to a dirty window and clean it close-up. Press V or the Camera button any time to switch to third person (GTA-style, seeing the cleaner from a distance) and back.

Desktop: W A S D or arrow keys to walk, drag the mouse on the 3D view to look (click it to lock the mouse), V for camera. Phone: drag the left joystick to walk, drag the right look area — or drag right on the 3D view itself — to look, and tap a window in 3D or in the 2D strip below to walk up to it. Then use the big cleaning buttons in order.
